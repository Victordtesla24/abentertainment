## Quick Brand Reference

**Brand:** AB Entertainment — ultra-premium cinematic event brand  
**Vibe:** Black + gold, theatrical luxury, Game of Thrones cinematic meets Fortune 500 events  
**Fonts:** Playfair Display (display/headings) + DM Sans (body/UI)  
**Colors:** `#0A0A0A` bg · `#C9A84C` gold · `#D4B65C` gold-light · `rgba(255,255,255,0.5)` muted text  
**Buttons:** sharp edges (0px radius), gold gradient primary, gold outline secondary  
**Cards:** glass morphism — `rgba(255,255,255,0.02)` bg, `rgba(201,168,76,0.08)` border, `blur(12px)`  
**Motion:** `cubic-bezier(0.25,1,0.5,1)` everywhere, Framer Motion entrances  
**Copy tone:** formal, theatrical, no emoji, ALL CAPS display headings, gold eyebrow labels
